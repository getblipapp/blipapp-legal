Blip Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Blip (“Blip”, “we”, “us”) is operated by Sufyan. This policy explains what information the Blip app collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. Questions: getblipapp@gmail.com.

In short: we collect only what is needed to run the app. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers. You can delete your account and its data at any time inside the app.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address (email or Google sign-up), or your phone number (phone sign-up), a password, and a username. Your username is visible to other users.
  • Sign in with Google: we verify your Google sign-in and receive your Google email address. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or other Google data.
  • Content you create: photo Blips you send, short videos (“reels”) you post, chat messages, your friend connections and your streak counts.
  • Reports and blocks: when you report someone we store your report, the reason and details you provide, the reported account and, for reports from a chat, up to the 20 most recent messages exchanged between you and that account. We also store who you have blocked.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifier: your Android device ID. We never store it in readable form; it is converted to a one-way keyed hash and used only to allow one account per device and to prevent abuse.
  • Technical logs: our hosting providers keep standard logs such as IP address, request time, app version and error details for security and troubleshooting.

Device permissions

  • Camera and microphone: used only when you take a photo or record a reel. Media stays on your device until you choose to send or post it.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, your advertising ID, payment information or biometric data.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, including sending and checking verification codes.
  • To deliver Blips, chats and reels to the people you choose, and to calculate streaks.
  • To prevent abuse and spam, enforce one account per device, and apply rate limits.
  • To review reports, enforce our Terms of Service and keep users safe.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.
  • To fix problems and keep the service running.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal effects on you. We do not read the content of your Blips and chats except when needed to investigate a report, protect the security of the service, provide support you request, or comply with the law.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (running your account and the features you use), our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention and safety), compliance with legal obligations, and your consent (camera and microphone permissions, which you can withdraw in your device settings).

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • Other users. Your username, friend connection and streak count are visible to your friends. Blips and chats are visible to the people you send them to. Reels are visible to other signed-in Blip users. Other users can save or screenshot what you send; we cannot prevent that.
  • Service providers that process data for us:
    • Supabase: hosting, database, file storage, authentication and server functions.
    • Brevo: sends email verification codes (receives your email address and the code).
    • Twilio: sends SMS or WhatsApp verification codes, only if you sign up with a phone number (receives your phone number and the code).
    • Google: sign-in with Google and Google Play services on your device.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to protect the rights, safety or property of users or the public, or to investigate abuse. We report apparent child sexual abuse material and other illegal content to the competent authorities, such as NCMEC or law enforcement, where the law requires or permits.
  • Business changes. If Blip is transferred to another operator, your information may transfer with it under the same protections.

5. International transfers

Your information is stored with our hosting provider in the cloud region selected for the Blip project and may be processed in other countries, including the United States, by the providers listed above. These providers use contractual safeguards for international transfers where required.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Passwords, verification codes and device identifiers are stored only as hashes. Database access is restricted by row-level security so users can read only data addressed to them. Chats and Blips are not end-to-end encrypted. No online service is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, open your profile and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, friendships, Blips, chats, reels, files and device binding. See how deletion works.
  • Block and report: you can block or report any user or reel from within the app. Blocked users cannot contact you, and you can manage blocked accounts in your profile.
  • Permissions: you can revoke camera and microphone access at any time in your Android settings.
  • Access, correction, portability, restriction and objection: email getblipapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify that the request comes from you.
  • EEA, UK and similar regions: you may have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California and similar laws: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not discriminate against users who exercise their privacy rights.

8. Children

Blip is not for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country to use online services without parental consent if that age is higher. We do not knowingly collect information from children below that age. If you believe a child has created an account, email getblipapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make material changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date above shows the latest version.
