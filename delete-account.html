Delete your Blip account

Effective November 1, 2026

In the app

  1. Open Blip and tap your avatar to open your profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app

Email getblipapp@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with (or tell us your username and the phone number you signed up with) and ask us to delete your account. We will process it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, username and sign-in details.
  • Your friendships, streaks and block list.
  • Blips you sent and files of unopened Blips sent to you.
  • Your chat messages.
  • Your reels and their video files.
  • The hashed device identifier that links your device to your account.

What may be kept

  • Reports filed by you or about you, including attached messages, for up to 12 months (longer if an investigation is open or the law requires), to protect other users.
  • Copies in provider backups, which are overwritten after a limited time.
  • Information we are legally required to keep.
